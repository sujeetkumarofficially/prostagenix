Return and Refund Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

This Return and Refund Policy explains the conditions and procedures for requesting a return, replacement, or refund for Prostagenix Capsules ordered through prostagenix.co.ke.

This website provides information about Prostagenix Capsules for adults in Kenya.

Our customer support team may contact you by telephone to confirm your order details, answer your questions, and explain the applicable price, package options, and delivery arrangements.

Please review these details carefully before confirming your order to help avoid misunderstandings.

You may request a return or refund within 4 days of receiving your order, subject to the conditions outlined in this policy.

5Return Conditions
